# Refund Policy

**Last updated:** September 8, 2026

This Refund Policy applies to subscriptions purchased through Rolewire.

## 1. Merchant of Record

Purchases made via card or PayPal on rolewire.insan.qzz.io are processed by **Paddle.com, acting as Merchant of Record**. Paddle handles billing, invoicing, and refund processing for these transactions in accordance with Paddle's own [Refund Policy](https://www.paddle.com/legal/refund-policy) and [Buyer Terms](https://www.paddle.com/legal/buyer-terms), which apply alongside this policy.

Purchases made via UddoktaPay (bKash/Nagad, Bangladesh) are processed directly by InsanLab, and the terms below apply to those transactions.

## 2. 14-Day Refund Window

If you're unhappy with Rolewire, you can request a full refund within **14 days** of your initial purchase or of a plan upgrade, no questions asked.

To request a refund:
- **Card/PayPal purchases:** contact Paddle directly via the link in your receipt/invoice, or email **support@rolewire.io** and we'll help coordinate with Paddle.
- **UddoktaPay purchases:** email **support@rolewire.io** with your account email and payment reference.

## 3. After the 14-Day Window

Refund requests made after the first 14 days are reviewed on a case-by-case basis — for example, in the case of billing errors, duplicate charges, or extended service outages. Renewal charges (monthly or annual) are not automatically refunded after this window, but you can cancel at any time to prevent future renewals.

## 4. Cancellations

You can cancel your subscription at any time from your account billing settings. Cancellation stops future renewals but does not automatically refund the current billing period unless it falls within the 14-day window above.

## 5. Processing Time

Approved refunds are processed to your original payment method and typically appear within 5–14 business days, depending on your bank or payment provider.

## 6. Contact

For any refund or billing question, contact **support@rolewire.io**.
