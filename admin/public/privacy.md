# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how InsanLab ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use Rolewire (the "Service").

## 1. Who This Applies To

This policy covers two groups of people:

- **Customers** — businesses and their staff who sign up for a Rolewire account to post jobs and manage applications.
- **Applicants** — individuals who apply to jobs posted through a Customer's career page, via Rolewire's public API.

## 2. Information We Collect

**From Customers:**
- Account information: name, work email, password (hashed) or OAuth identity (Google, GitHub, LinkedIn, Facebook)
- Company information: company name, career page slug
- Billing information: handled directly by Paddle (our payment processor and Merchant of Record) or UddoktaPay — we do not store your card details ourselves
- Storage credentials you provide (S3, Google Drive, Telegram), which are encrypted at rest

**From Applicants (on behalf of Customers):**
- Information submitted through a job application: name, contact details, resume/CV, and any custom question responses the Customer has configured
- We do not control what fields a Customer collects; Customers are responsible for the questions they ask applicants

**Automatically:**
- Basic technical logs (IP address hashes used transiently for rate-limiting; not persisted)

## 3. How We Use Information

- To provide and operate the Service (authentication, job posting, application delivery)
- To process payments (via Paddle/UddoktaPay) and send billing-related emails (receipts, renewal reminders, payment-failed notices)
- To send transactional email (OTP/login, application-received confirmations to applicants, product notices)
- To maintain security and prevent abuse of public API endpoints

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use applicant data for advertising.

## 4. Who We Share Information With

- **Paddle.com**, as Merchant of Record, for payment processing, tax compliance, and billing support (global card/PayPal payments)
- **UddoktaPay**, for Bangladesh-market payments (bKash/Nagad)
- **Storage providers you configure** (AWS S3, Google Drive, Telegram) — when a Customer chooses "bring your own storage," applicant files go directly to that Customer's own storage, and the Customer is the data controller for that data
- We do not share data with third parties for marketing purposes

## 5. Data Retention

We retain account and application data for as long as an account is active, plus a reasonable period after cancellation for legal and billing record-keeping. You can request deletion by contacting us (see below).

## 6. Data Security

We use encryption at rest for sensitive configuration data (including stored storage credentials), and take reasonable technical measures to protect data in transit (TLS) and at rest. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact **support@rolewire.io**.

If you are an applicant and have a question about how your data was collected, please contact the business you applied to directly — they control what data is collected on their career page. We can help route requests to the responsible Customer where needed.

## 8. International Transfers

Rolewire's infrastructure and sub-processors (including Paddle) may process data in countries other than your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate legal safeguards for such transfers.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Rolewire is not directed at individuals under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated by email or in-app notice.

## 11. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to **support@rolewire.io**.
